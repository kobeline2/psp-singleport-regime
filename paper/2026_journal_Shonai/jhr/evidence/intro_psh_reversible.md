### 1.0 Flow-direction mapping (read before using any I-4 row)

This paper: inflow = water enters the basin through the port (pumping mode, because the basin stands for an upper reservoir); outflow = water leaves the basin (generating mode) (CONV L110). Several works below concern a LOWER reservoir, where the mode labels are reversed. Write by flow direction (discharge into the reservoir / withdrawal from it), not by "pumping" or "generating".
